House rules

Things your letter will never say.

“I am writing to express my interest…”

Generic openings are banned. The first line names something real about the role and connects it to you.

“I am passionate and results-driven.”

Empty claims get flagged and rewritten. Specific things you did stand in for adjectives about yourself.

“I grew revenue by 300%.”

Unless you did. Nothing is invented: every number, project, and skill comes from your own documents.

Questions

Before you upload.

What do I need to get started?

Your resume and one to five cover letters you've written before. The letters teach it how you write; the resume gives it the facts it's allowed to use.

Will it make things up about me?

No. Every project, number, and skill in a draft has to come from your own documents. Before you see a draft, it's checked against them, and anything it can't back up gets rewritten.

What happens to my documents?

They're stored in your account and used only to write your letters. You can delete any upload from your profile at any time. We never sell your data or use it to train AI models. The privacy policy lists every service that touches it.

Is it free?

Yes, while we're in beta. If that ever changes, we'll tell you well in advance, and nothing you've already written goes behind a paywall.

How many times can I revise a letter?

Up to three revisions per letter. Tell it what to change in plain words, like “warmer opening” or “mention the Kafka project”, then accept the version you'd send.

Which languages does it write in?

English today. German is next on the list.
